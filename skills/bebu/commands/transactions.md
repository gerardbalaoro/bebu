# `bebu transactions`

Read history and write one-off expenses or income

## Subcommands

- [`create`](transactions/create.md) - Create an expense or income with all ledger changes. The
  command does not allocate funds automatically
- [`delete`](transactions/delete.md) - Delete a supported transaction and reverse its ledger effects
  atomically
- [`list`](transactions/list.md) - Read the complete history, including older records absent from
  the app cache
- [`show`](transactions/show.md) - Read a complete transaction record
- [`update`](transactions/update.md) - Change notes, color, or cleared status in the record and its
  indexes
